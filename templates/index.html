Understanding Eligibility

Why Loan Eligibility Matters

Lenders evaluate multiple factors when assessing a loan application. Understanding these factors helps you prepare a stronger profile and make informed borrowing decisions.

💰

Income & Stability

Your annual income and employment history demonstrate your ability to make consistent repayments over the loan term.

📊

Credit Score

A strong credit score reflects responsible credit use and signals to lenders that you are a lower-risk borrower.

🏠

Home Ownership

Whether you rent, own, or have a mortgage provides lenders with insight into your financial stability and assets.

📉

Debt-to-Income Ratio

The ratio of your loan amount to income helps lenders gauge whether the requested amount is manageable for you.

📅

Credit History Length

A longer credit history gives lenders more data to assess your repayment patterns and financial behaviour over time.

⚠️

Past Defaults

Any previous loan defaults significantly affect eligibility, as they indicate higher risk to potential lenders.

Smart Borrowing

Responsible Borrowing Tips

Before taking on any debt, consider these principles to protect your financial well-being.

1

Borrow only what you need and can comfortably repay. Avoid overextending your monthly budget with loan obligations.

2

Review your credit report regularly for accuracy. Errors on your report can unfairly lower your score and limit your options.

3

Compare offers from multiple lenders before committing. Interest rates and terms can vary significantly across providers.

4

Consider the total cost of the loan, not just the monthly payment. Factor in interest paid over the entire loan term.

5

Build an emergency fund before taking on debt. Unexpected expenses should not force you into missed payments.

6

Understand all terms and conditions. Read the fine print on fees, penalties, and variable rate clauses before signing.
